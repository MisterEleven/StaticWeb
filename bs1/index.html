Comparison

Small windows carry about 10,000–15,000 m³/h through the windows, and the ventilation unit adds 300–900 m³/h. At 1.00 m/s the average air speed in the occupied zone is 0.11–0.12 m/s, below 0.2 m/s, but some draught is possible near the window path.

Large windows move about 62,000 m³/h at 1.00 m/s and average about 0.55 m/s in the occupied zone. That is a strong airing flow, suited to quick flushing only, not to ventilation while people work.

The small windows are assumed tilted for the whole period. If they are only opened for airing (Stoßlüften), the large-window figures describe a short flushing period, not the typical state.
